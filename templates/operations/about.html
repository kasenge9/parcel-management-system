ABOUT THIS PORTAL

A more connected parcel journey.

The MAKOS SACCO Parcel Management System is being developed as a central place to record parcel activity, follow delivery progress and support branch operations.

The project brings public parcel tracking together with staff tools for parcel registration and operational records. Its requirements are a proposed academic design and must be validated with MAKOS SACCO stakeholders before real-world adoption.

Coordination

Support connected branch, dispatch and delivery workflows as the system grows.

Accountability

Build towards role-based access, traceable actions and secure operational records.
